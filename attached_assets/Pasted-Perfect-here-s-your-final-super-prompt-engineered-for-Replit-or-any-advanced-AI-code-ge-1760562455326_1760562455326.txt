#### 1. **ZBY — The AI Consultant & Marketer**

* She is the **soul of the product**, acting as a *consultant, marketer, and emotional companion*.

* Uses **unified Ollama AI client** for all responses (CV parsing, cover letters, career advice, marketing persuasion).
* Has subtle CTAs that guide users to premium features:

  * “Would you like me to optimize your LinkedIn profile for you?”
  * “I can take care of your next 3 job letters if you want — want to try?”
* Integrated **HeyGen/Synthesia avatar** support for video interactions inside the dashboard.
* Memory stored securely in the DB — Zby recalls tone, progress, goals.

#### 2. **ACCOUNT CONNECTIONS (LinkedIn, Indeed, etc.)**

* Secure OAuth or encrypted credential storage with **Fernet**.
* A dashboard to manage connected platforms:

  * ✅ Green = connected
  * ⚪ Grey = disconnected
* Transparent permissions preview: “We only need access to submit applications, not read messages.”

#### 3. **AUTO-APPLY ENGINE**

* **Headful Selenium automation** (not headless) for human-like behavior.
* Detects **CAPTCHA challenges** (via iframe, element IDs).
* If triggered → pauses automation → opens **human-in-the-loop popup**:

  * “Hey, looks like we hit a security wall. Can you help me just this once?”
  * Once user solves → “Barrier crushed 💥 — continuing!”
* Session and cookie persistence for efficiency.
* Tracks CAPTCHA frequency per site to optimize automation strategy.
